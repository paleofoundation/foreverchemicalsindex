# Methodology

Evidence is published only after primary-source verification, provenance capture, and review of its fitness for the claim. Every measurement must identify its source page, product, analyte, unit, basis, and location in the source.

A source needs a verified DOI or an explicit `no_doi_assigned` declaration with an access URL. Non-detects, reporting limits, and measured values remain distinct. Aggregate fluorine measurements must not be presented as individual PFAS concentrations.

Empty fields are gaps. Category stubs are not completed ingests. Regulatory limits require the applicable primary instrument and verified scope.
