# Development and Application of a Liquid Chromatography-Tandem Mass Spectrometry Method for the Analysis of 20 Perfluoroalkyl Substances in Fruit and Vegetables at Sub-Parts-per-Trillion Levels

## Summary

Validated LC-MS/MS method for 20 PFAS at sub-ppt level in fruit and vegetables (LOQ down to 0.5 ng/kg, apparent recovery 90 to 119%), applied to 215 fruit and vegetable samples from grocery stores and markets; leafy vegetables the main contributor, mainly PFOA, then PFHpA and PFHxA.
