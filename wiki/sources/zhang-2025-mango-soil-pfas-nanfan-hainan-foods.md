# Occurrence, Sources, and Risk Assessment of PFAS in Soil-Mango Systems of the Chinese Tropical Nanfan District

## Summary

PFAS in soils and mangoes in the Nanfan District, Hainan, China; UPLC-MS/MS. Soil sum 0.18 to 1.07 ng/g; mango sum 0.0019 to 0.0201 ng/g wet weight, PFHxA 44% and PFHpA 30% of the sum; BAF below 1; source attribution and risk.
