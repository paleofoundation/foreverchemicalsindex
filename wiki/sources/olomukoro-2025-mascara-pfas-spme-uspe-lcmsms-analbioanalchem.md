# Evaluation of extraction methodologies for PFAS analysis in mascara: a comparative study of SPME and automated µSPE

## Summary

Method comparison (SPME vs automated micro-SPE) for eight anionic PFAS in mascara, quantified by LC-MS/MS; applied to six mascara products (waterproof and non-waterproof).
