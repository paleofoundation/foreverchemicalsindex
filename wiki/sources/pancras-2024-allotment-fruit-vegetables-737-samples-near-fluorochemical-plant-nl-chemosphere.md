# Large scale study on PFASs levels in fruits, vegetables and soil from allotments and gardens contaminated by atmospheric deposition from a Dutch fluorochemical production plant

## Summary

737 fruit and vegetable samples from 17 allotments and 4 gardens near a fluorochemical production plant (Dordrecht, Netherlands), plus soils; targeted UPLC-MS/MS. PFOA and HFPO-DA dominant; levels highest directly downwind; brassicas, leafy and root vegetables highest; PFOA BAFs about 10-fold below literature.
