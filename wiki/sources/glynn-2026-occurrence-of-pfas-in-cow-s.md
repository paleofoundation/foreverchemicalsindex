## Methods (brief)

Farm milk: acid precipitation, weak anion exchange SPE, graphitized-carbon clean-up and UPLC-MS/MS MRM. Facility milk: QuEChERS, dispersive SPE and LC-MS/MS (PDF pp.3 to 4).

Targets as printed or genericized: Initial farm panel: PFCA C4 to C14; PFBS; PFHxS; PFOS isomers; PFDS; FOSA. Final milk and facility target lists require absent supplementary Tables S3 to S6. Verified main milk targets: PFOA; PFNA; PFHxS; PFOS (PDF pp.3 to 6).


## Limitations
