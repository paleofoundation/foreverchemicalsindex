## Evidence Fitness

## Methods (brief)

SPE LC-MS/MS based on EPA Method 537.1, PDF pp. 5-7. Single extraction and instrumental analysis per retail sample.

Target labels as printed: PFHxA; NMeFOSAA; PFHpA; PFOA; PFUnA; PFDoA; PFNA; PFDA; PFTA; PFTrDA (Tables 3-5).

Detection and quantification limits: Table 4, PDF p. 10: PFOA LOD 0.05 ng/L, LOQ 0.16 ng/L. Method-level limits. Table 5 control results are Not Detected; these controls are not bottled-product occurrence rows.


## Limitations

Appendix A is cited but absent from the deposited PDF. Elevated result not confirmed by repeat extraction or confirmatory injection (PDF pp. 11-12). Variable recoveries limit quantification. Title page publication precedes acceptance as printed; dates not repaired. MANIFEST includes PFHxS/PFOS, which are absent from the stated target set.
