## Evidence Fitness



## Methods (brief)

Modified QuEChERS extraction and UHPLC-MS/MS, negative ESI with dynamic multiple reaction monitoring (PDF pp.4 to 7, sections 2.3 to 2.4).

Targets as printed or genericized: PFBA; PFPeA; PFHxA; PFHpA; PFOA; PFNA; PFDA; PFUnDA; PFDoDA; PFTrDA; PFTeDA; PFBS; PFPeS; PFHxS; PFHpS; PFOS isomers; PFNS; PFDS; PFUnDS; PFDoDS; PFTrDS; FOSA; DONA; HFPO-DA; 4:2 FTS; 6:2 FTS; 8:2 FTS; 9Cl-PF3ONS; 11Cl-PF3OUdS; additional sulfonamide standards with commercial identifiers omitted (PDF pp.4,6 to 7).


## Limitations

LB and UB statistics must not be collapsed. Table and narrative PFOS descriptions differ; MISMATCHES.csv preserves the conflict. PFBA and PFPeA did not meet the confirmation criterion (PDF p.9). No conversions, risk thresholds, representativeness claims, or safety conclusions are added.
