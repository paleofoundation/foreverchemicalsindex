## Evidence Fitness

primary_measurement. Table 3 provides both area and mass units; selected rows retain the area basis. Volatile-analyte recovery issues and a PAP prose/table conflict are held.


## Methods (brief)

Methanol ultrasonic extraction followed by UPLC-MS/MS. Twenty analytes and isotope standards listed in Table 1, PDF p.4; sample results in Table 3, PDF p.11.

Analytes or measurement scopes: PFBA, PFPeA, PFHxA, PFOA, PFDA, PFUnDA, PFDoDA, PFHxDA, PFBS, PFHxS, PFOS, PFOSA, N-MeFOSA, 4:2 FTOH, 6:2 FTOH, 8:2 FTOH, 6:2 PAP, 8:2 PAP, 6:2 diPAP, 8:2 diPAP. Target identities are not inferred from the manifest acronym scan.


## Limitations
