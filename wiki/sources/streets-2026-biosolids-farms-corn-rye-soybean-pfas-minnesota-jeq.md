# Per‐ and polyfluoroalkyl substances in land‐applied biosolids: Accumulation in soils, crop uptake, and potential dietary risk

## Summary

Three Minnesota farms with fields with no, past, or current-year biosolids application: biosolids, soils, corn (grain and stover), rye and soybeans analysed for PFAS by LC-MS/MS; PFOS and others accumulated mainly in corn stover; PFAS also found in rye.
