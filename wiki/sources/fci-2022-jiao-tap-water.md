# A pilot study on extractable organofluorine and per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS) in water from drinking water treatment plants around Taihu Lake, China: what is missed by target PFAS analysis?


## Summary

Raw and treated drinking-water samples have targeted PFAS, suspect screening and extractable organofluorine assays. EOF is an aggregate fluorine measure and is not interchangeable with any individual PFAS concentration.
