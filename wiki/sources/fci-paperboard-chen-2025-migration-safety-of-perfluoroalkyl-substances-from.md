## Evidence Fitness

primary_measurement. Pulp tableware is adjacent packaging evidence, not a carton-specific estimate. Residue and migration units and limits differ. Safety and regulatory claims are not adopted.


## Methods (brief)

LC-MS/MS target list PFOA, PFOS, PFNA, PFHxA, PFPeA (PDF p.4, Table 3). TF and EOF determined with ion-selective electrode (PDF pp.2-3). Conditions vary by container and simulant (Table 6, PDF p.7).

Analytes or measurement scopes: PFOA, PFOS, PFNA, PFHxA, PFPeA, TF, EOF. Target identities are not inferred from the manifest acronym scan.


## Limitations

Residue PFOA LOD 0.2 and LOQ 0.5 µg/L; PFOS LOD 0.5 and LOQ 1.2 µg/L (Table 3, PDF p.4). Migration Figure 3 states PFOA/PFPeA LOQ ~0.5 µg/kg and PFOS/PFNA/PFHxA ~0.5-3 µg/kg (PDF p.7).

Selected rows are a bounded extraction, not a complete transcription of every table. Supplied main articles are the evidence boundary; unstaged supplements were not retrieved.
