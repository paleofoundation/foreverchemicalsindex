## Methods (brief)

PFAS extraction using QuEChERS and dispersive SPE; HPLC-MS/MS/UPLC-MS/MS triple quadrupole methodology (PDF p.7 and pp.14 to 16).

Targets as printed or genericized: Occurrence Table 1 lists PFOA; PFBA; PFPHpA as printed; PFDA; PFBS. Complete panel and analytical parameters are in absent supplementary Tables S1 and S9 (PDF pp.7,9)..


## Limitations

Table 1 dry-weight reporting is kept literal; samples include powder and liquid formulations and powder reconstitution is described on PDF p.14. The printed PFPHpA identity is unresolved and not routed. PFBA is documented as a target but has no dedicated scaffold route; its result is not included in these selected rows. No per-formulation result is inferred from the pooled summary. No conversions, risk thresholds, representativeness claims, or safety conclusions are added.
