## Evidence Fitness



## Methods (brief)

Acetonitrile extraction/protein precipitation and SPE; UPLC-HRMS negative ESI, full scan with data-independent confirmatory acquisition (PDF p.4 sections 2.3 to 2.4).

Targets as printed or genericized: PFBA; PFPeA; PFHxA; PFHpA; PFOA; PFNA; PFDA; FOUEA; NaDONA; PFBS; PFHxS; PFOS; N-MeFOSAA; 6:2 FTS (PDF p.3 and p.6 Table 2).


## Limitations

Numerical statistics below LOD and N.D. are both printed in Table 2; no censoring correction is applied here. The MANIFEST target list is incomplete. Cream is a laboratory-separated milk fraction, not retail butter. No conversions, risk thresholds, representativeness claims, or safety conclusions are added.
