## Methods (brief)

Off-line SPE HPLC-MS/MS with isotopically labelled surrogates and a separate total oxidizable precursor assay, PDF pp. 3-5. Targeted concentrations are not total organic fluorine.

Target labels as printed: PFBA; PFBS; PFDA; PFDoA; PFDoS; PFDS; PFHpA; PFHpS; PFHxA; PFHxS; PFNA; PFNS; PFOA; PFOS; PFOSA; PFPeA; PFPS; PFTrA; PFTrS; PFUnDA; PFUnS; 4:2 FTS; 6:2 FTS; 8:2 FTS; N-EtFOSAA; N-MetFOSAA (Table 3, PDF p. 8).

Detection and quantification limits: Table 3, PDF p. 8: LOD 0.1 ng/L for PFOA, PFNA, PFDA and PFDoA; 0.8 ng/L for PFNS and PFDS. LOQ is the lowest calibration point: 0.25 ng/L for the former group and 2.5 ng/L for the latter. PDF p. 7 reports overall LOQ range 0.25-5 ng/L. PDF p. 11 reports no target precursors detected in bottled water; no zero substituted.


## Limitations

Abstract p. 1 reports up to 12 ng/L total whereas body p. 10 reports 8 ng/L; both retained in the mismatch log, no reconciliation. The below-LOQ group is ambiguously described as seven compounds but only six names are printed. Sample-level table is not supplied. No tap-water results routed in this branch.
