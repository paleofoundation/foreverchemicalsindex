# Uptake and distribution of perfluoroalkyl substances by grafted tomato plants cultivated in a contaminated site in northern Italy

## Summary

Tomato plants with different rootstocks grown on PFAS-polluted soil and irrigated with PFAS-polluted groundwater (North-East Italy); leaves and fruits (1st and 6th truss) by LC-MS/MS; C4 to C8 PFCAs and PFSAs in leaves, only C4 to C6 carboxylic PFAS in fruits; dietary hazard quotients.
