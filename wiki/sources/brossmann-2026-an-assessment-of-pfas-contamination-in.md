# An assessment of PFAS contamination in wild boar meat and liver

## Summary

Per abstract: In this study 266 wild boar samples (152 meat and 114 liver) from Germany were evaluated for contamination with four EU-regulated perfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS), namely perfluorooctane sulfonic acid (PFOS), perfluorooctanoic acid (PFOA), perfluorononanoic acid (PFNA), and perfluorohexane sulfonic acid (PFHxS).
