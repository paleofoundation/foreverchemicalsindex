# Occurrence and Dietary Exposure of PFAS in Singapore: Insights from a Total Diet Study

## Summary

Singapore Total Diet Study: 503 pooled samples across 23 food categories (incl. cereals, vegetables, fruit) as consumed, analysed for PFOS, PFOA, PFNA and PFHxS by LC-MS/MS; 75% of categories non-detect; fish and seafood 89.7% of detections.
