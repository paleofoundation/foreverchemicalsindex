## Methods (brief)

Weak-anion-exchange SPE and LC-MS/MS, negative ESI MRM, PDF pp. 2-3. Validation refers to EPA methods; storage deviates from EPA 537.1 guidance as acknowledged in section 2.3. Acidic-form concentrations.

Target labels as printed: PFPeA; PFHxA; PFHpA; PFOA; PFNA; PFDA; PFUnA; PFDoA; PFTriA; PFTeDA; PFBS; PFHxS; PFOS; HFPO-DA (Tables 1 and section 2.2).

Detection and quantification limits: MDLs 0.06-0.11 ng/L, PDF p. 3, section 2.6; analyte-specific Table S8 absent. Table 1 ND retained, never zero. Quantification limits not supplied in main text.


## Limitations

Supplementary information absent; individual MDLs cannot be assigned. Table 1 reports minimum PFNA and summed detections 0.05 ng/L despite main-text MDL range starting at 0.06 ng/L; log retained. Abstract range agrees with the summed range, not individual-analyte means. PFBA is explicitly excluded in section 2.1 despite MANIFEST including it.
