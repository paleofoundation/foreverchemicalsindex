# Crop bioaccumulation and human exposure of perfluoroalkyl acids through multi-media transport from a mega fluorochemical industrial park, China

## Summary

Multi-media PFAA contamination within 10 km of a mega fluorochemical industrial park (Shandong, China): soil, precipitation, wheat grain and maize grain; maxima 640 ng/g in wheat grain and 509 ng/g in maize grain; distance gradient; human exposure via grain.
