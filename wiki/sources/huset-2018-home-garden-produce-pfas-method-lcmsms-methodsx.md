# Quantitative determination of perfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS) in soil, water, and home garden produce

## Summary

Method article: isotope-dilution LC-ESI-MS/MS for C4 to C8 PFAS in water, soil and home-garden produce (dispersive SPE) from Minnesota homes with a history of PFAS-contaminated drinking water; method performance and QC.
