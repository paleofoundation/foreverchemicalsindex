## Methods (brief)

LC-MS/MS with isotope-labeled internal standards; extraction and solid-phase cleanup (PDF pp.3 to 4).

Targets as printed or genericized: PFBS; PFPeS; PFHxS; PFHpS; linear and branched PFOS; PFHxA; PFHpA; PFOA; PFNA; PFDA; PFUnDA; PFDoA; PFTrDA; PFTeDA (PDF p.2 analytes of interest).


## Limitations

Goat and sheep results are excluded from this cell. Source individual-analyte means should not be summed to reconstruct lower-bound totals. No total fluorine measurement is supported by the methods. No conversions, risk thresholds, representativeness claims, or safety conclusions are added.
