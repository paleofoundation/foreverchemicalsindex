# Effects of Nitrogen Fertilizer Types on the Uptake and Translocation of PFAS and Metabolomic Activities of Hydroponically Cultivated Lettuce (Lactuca sativa)

## Summary

Hydroponic lettuce in a growth chamber exposed to PFOA, PFOS, HFPO-DA and 6:2 Cl-PFESA with three nitrogen fertilizers; PFAS in shoots and roots (triple-quadrupole LC-MS/MS) plus metabolomics; ammonium sulfate raised HFPO-DA shoot uptake about 108%, KNO3 raised PFOS 267% and 6:2 Cl-PFESA 395%.
