# Extractable Per- and Polyfluoroalkyl Substances in Menstrual Underwear

## Summary

Three menstrual underwear garments plus one non-menstrual organic cotton underwear (control), separated into layers; methanol extracts analysed by targeted LC-MS/MS. Twenty target PFAS above LOQ in at least one layer; total target PFAS up to 1.69 ng/g fabric; outer crotch layer highest; inner gusset also positive; fluorotelomers most frequently detected.
