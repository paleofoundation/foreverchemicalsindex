## Methods (brief)

SPE with weak-anion-exchange sorbent followed by LC-MS/MS in MRM mode, PDF pp. 3-4. The EPA Method 533 native analyte standard is named; this alone does not establish full method compliance.

Target labels as printed: PFBA; PFBS; PFHxA; PFPeA; PFHpA; PFHxS; PFHpS; PFOA; PFNA; PFOS; PFDA; PFNS; PFDoDA; PFDS; PFUnDA (Table 1); PFMPA; 4:2 FTSA; HFPO-DA; NFDHA; PFEESA; PFMBA; DONA; 6:2 FTSA; 9Cl-PF3ONS; 8:2 FTSA; 11Cl-PF3OUdS; PFOSA (Table 2).

Detection and quantification limits: LOD 0.02 ng/L and LOQ 0.06 ng/L for all targeted compounds, PDF p. 5, section 2.5. Table 1 <LOQ is retained.


## Limitations

Supplementary Tables S1-S11 are not deposited. Abstract composition proportions differ from the tabulated regulated/unregulated means when interpreted as the same denominator, so no proportions are routed. Table means have unspecified nondetect treatment. All tap-water and risk calculations excluded.
