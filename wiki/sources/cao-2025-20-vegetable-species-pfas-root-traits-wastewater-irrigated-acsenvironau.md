# Linking Vegetable Per- and Polyfluoroalkyl Substance Accumulation with Root Chemical Traits

## Summary

PFAS in 20 vegetable species grown on farmland irrigated with treated wastewater; leafy vegetables (lettuce, spinach) mean 9.24 ng/g vs lower in carrot; PFBA dominant in all vegetables; links to root chemical traits.
