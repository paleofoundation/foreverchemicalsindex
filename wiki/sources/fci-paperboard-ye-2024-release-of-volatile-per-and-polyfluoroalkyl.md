## Evidence Fitness

primary_measurement. Landfill release is not food migration or initial packaging concentration. Terminated Popcorn Bags 1 reactor is distinct from completed reactors (PDF p.5).


## Methods (brief)

PIGE screening followed by volatile PFAS gas analysis using thermal desorption GC-MS (PDF pp.2-4). Table 2 lists FTOHs, FTOs, FTIs, FTAcs, FTMAcs and sulfonamide derivatives.

Analytes or measurement scopes: 6:2 FTOH, volatile PFAS. Target identities are not inferred from the manifest acronym scan.


## Limitations
