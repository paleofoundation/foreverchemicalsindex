# Analyte and matrix method extension of per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances in food and feed

## Summary

FDA single-lab validation extending method C-010.02 to 30 PFAS in seven foods and two feeds (matrices include lettuce, rice, chocolate, among others) by LC-MS/MS; assesses LOQs vs EURL POPs guidance and EU maximum levels.
