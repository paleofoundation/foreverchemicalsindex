## Methods (brief)

Simplified QuEChERS without dispersive SPE; UHPLC-MS/MS negative ESI triple-quadrupole time-managed MRM (PDF p.13 sections 4.2.3 and 4.3).

Targets as printed or genericized: L-PFBS; PFBA; PFDA; PFDoA; PFDS; PFHpA; PFHxA; PFHxS; PFNA; PFOA; PFOS; PFPeA; PFTeDA; PFTrDA; PFuDA (PDF p.4 Table 1; names p.12).


## Limitations

Per-sample occurrence Table S3 is absent; the body supports only limited formula-specific extraction. Mass basis is printed ng kg−1, with no ready-to-feed or dry-basis conversion inferred. Breastmilk reference-material results are not infant-formula findings. No conversions, risk thresholds, representativeness claims, or safety conclusions are added.
