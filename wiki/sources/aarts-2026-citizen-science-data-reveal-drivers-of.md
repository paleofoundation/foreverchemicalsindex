## Evidence Fitness



## Methods (brief)

Citizen-submitted accredited laboratory reports; full extraction and instrument protocols unavailable for all laboratories (PDF pp.3 to 5). No LC-MS/MS method is inferred.

Targets as printed or genericized: PFOS; PFOA; PFNA; PFHxS (PDF p.4 Table 1).


## Limitations

Voluntary submissions and repeat sites limit independence. A maximum-value conflict is retained, not resolved. Laboratory and testing-site names are replaced by generic descriptors. No conversions, risk thresholds, representativeness claims, or safety conclusions are added.
