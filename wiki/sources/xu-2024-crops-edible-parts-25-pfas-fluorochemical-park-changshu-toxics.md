# Crop Contamination and Human Exposure to Per- and Polyfluoroalkyl Substances around a Fluorochemical Industrial Park in China

## Summary

25 PFAS (14 PFAAs, 2 precursors, 9 novel alternatives) in agricultural soils and edible parts of crops (leafy vegetables, Solanaceae, cereals and others) around a fluorochemical industrial park in Changshu, China; UPLC-MS/MS. Sum PFAS in edible parts 11.64 to 299.5 ng/g; PFBA about 71% of the sum; N-MeFOSAA in all crops.
